Featured River Adventures

Family Scenic Run

Perfect for beginners, families, and first-time rafters.

Enjoy a relaxing half-day journey down scenic river stretches with mild Class I & II rapids. Our experienced guides focus on safety, local wildlife history, and fun swimming stops.

The Whitewater Express

High energy, thrilling rapids, and action-packed fun.

Navigate thrilling Class III & IV rapids on this exciting full-day excursion. Includes a riverside lunch, safety gear, and experienced trip leaders to guide you through fast-paced drops.

Extreme Canyon Expedition

Designed specifically for seasoned thrill-seekers.

Tackle challenging Class V rapids deep within the canyon. This multi-day expedition includes complete river gear, backcountry meals, and technical navigation by our elite guides
